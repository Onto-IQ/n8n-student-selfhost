Job Description
Title : Senior Data Analyst

Job Summary:
As a Senior Data Analyst, you will play a crucial role in analyzing complex datasets to provide actionable insights that support strategic business decisions. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams, including marketing, finance, operations, and product development, to deliver data-driven solutions that enhance performance and drive growth.

Key Responsibilities:

Collect, analyze, and interpret large datasets from multiple sources to generate insights that inform business strategy.
Develop and maintain advanced data models, dashboards, and reports using tools such as Power BI, Tableau, and Excel.
Conduct deep-dive analyses to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities for optimization across various business functions.
Collaborate with stakeholders to understand their data needs and deliver customized solutions that drive business value.
Provide guidance and mentorship to junior data analysts and team members, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement.
Ensure data quality, accuracy, and consistency across all analytical outputs.
Communicate complex data insights in a clear and actionable manner to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Stay up-to-date with industry trends and emerging technologies in data analytics, and implement best practices to enhance the organization’s analytical capabilities.
Work closely with IT and data engineering teams to ensure seamless data integration and accessibility.
Qualifications:

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or a related field.
Minimum of 5-7 years of experience in data analysis, with a strong track record of delivering impactful insights.
Proficiency in data analysis tools such as SQL, R, Python, and advanced Excel.
Experience with data visualization tools like Power BI, Tableau, or similar platforms.
Strong understanding of statistical methods and data modeling techniques.
Excellent problem-solving skills with the ability to think critically and analytically.
Strong communication skills, with the ability to convey complex findings to a non-technical audience.
Experience in mentoring or leading a team of analysts is preferred.
Ability to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
Preferred Skills:

Experience in [specific industry or sector, e.g., finance, healthcare, e-commerce].
Familiarity with machine learning algorithms and predictive modeling.
Knowledge of cloud-based data platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
